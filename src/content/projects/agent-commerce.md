Ten stages, six kinds of agent, one facts ledger. A researcher identifies the exact factory
product and every reseller; a marketer builds buyer personas, a keyword set gated against real
autocomplete data, and a price plan from observed offers; a copywriter and a designer produce
the listing; a publisher pushes it through the marketplace API, preview first.

Seven of the ten stages need a human OK before they count as done.

The long write-up, with the lessons: [An agent team that ships Amazon listings](/blog/agent-team-amazon/).
